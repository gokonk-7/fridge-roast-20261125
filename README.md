One line, 155 characters, roasting a smart fridge — entered in make.fun's "Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh" bounty ($5). A house judge compares the lines two at a time and the funniest one takes the pool.

> My fridge updated its firmware overnight; now the ice maker has a login screen, a monthly subscription, and the confidence of a man who has never made ice.

The angle: a firmware update turns the ice maker into a smug middle manager — it gains a login screen and a subscription, and loses the one job it had. No grocery-ordering gag, no spying camera; just the specific smart-fridge pain of an update that makes the appliance worse.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later. The program writes the single roast line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 155 characters, inside the 200-character limit).
